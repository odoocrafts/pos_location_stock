POS Location Stock

Instantly check product stock across all warehouses and stores directly from the POS interface!

Demo: Checking stock in other warehouses seamlessly.

Key Features

  • Real-time Inventory Visibility: Cashiers can check product stock levels across all configured warehouses and internal locations without leaving the Point of Sale screen.
  • Streamlined Workflow: No need to open the backend inventory app or navigate complex menus. A single click from the POS control buttons gets the job done.
  • Boost Customer Experience: Quickly inform customers if a product is available at another store location or the main warehouse.
  • Clean, Intuitive UI: A lightweight, non-intrusive popup neatly presents stock grouped by location.
