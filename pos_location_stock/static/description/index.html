POS Location Stock

Instantly check product stock across all warehouses and stores directly from the POS interface!

Demo: Checking stock in other warehouses seamlessly.

Key Features

  • Real-time Inventory Visibility: Cashiers can check product stock levels across all configured warehouses and internal locations without leaving the Point of Sale screen.
  • Streamlined Workflow: No need to open the backend inventory app or navigate complex menus. A single click from the POS control buttons gets the job done.
  • Boost Customer Experience: Quickly inform customers if a product is available at another store location or the main warehouse.
  • Clean, Intuitive UI: A lightweight, non-intrusive popup neatly presents stock grouped by location.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

How do I check POS stock location for other warehouses?

With our POS Location Stock addon, simply select a product in the cart and click the "Check Stock" button. It instantly fetches and displays the stock quantities from all your other warehouses and internal locations.

Is this Point of Sale Stock Location app compatible with Odoo 19?

Yes! This Point of Sale multi-warehouse inventory tool is fully compatible with Odoo 19 Community and Enterprise editions.

Do I need to leave the POS screen to check multi-location inventory?

No. The main advantage of this POS Stock Location module is that cashiers can check real-time stock availability across different branches without ever leaving the checkout screen.
